Form
What is Form? Your overall performance — not just how often you win, but who you played, how convincingly, and how by much. Requires a minimum of 12 games to appear.

A quick guide to what each column means.

The columns explained

  • Player / Pair — the player's name, or the pairing.
  • Games — total games played (must reach 12 to appear).
  • Games Won — how many of those games were won.
  • Win % — share of games won.
  • Point Diff / Game — average point margin per game. 3.57 means winning games by about 3–4 points on average. Positive = usually wins convincingly; negative = usually loses.
  • Form — the overall performance score (typically 0.0–1.0). It is not a win percentage. The headline number the wall is sorted by.

Reading Form (typically 0.0 – 1.0)

It blends how often you win games (40%), your point margin per game (40%), and your points won per game (20%) — then adjusts for how many games you've played (more = more reliable). It is not the same as Win %: winning narrowly scores lower than winning convincingly. Rough guide:

  • 0.70+ — Strong, performing very well.
  • 0.55–0.70 — Good, winning more than losing.
  • 0.40–0.55 — Middling, competitive but mixed.
  • 0.00–0.40 — Struggling.
  • Below 0 — Losing sets by wide margins.

Why Form, not just Win %?

Win % treats a narrow 7–6 win the same as a crushing 6–0, and ignores who you played. Form rewards convincing performances and accounts for opponent strength — a fairer picture of how someone is actually playing. For doubles, Form shows how a partnership performs as a team.

    About OSR

    Open Squash Ratings

    Designed for All Sorts of Combined pairings

    What Is OSR?

    Open Squash Ratings (OSR) is an independent, transparent rating system designed for club squash — singles and doubles alike. Built on the proven OSR engine, OSR rates every player and every pairing on their own merits, for the realities of club squash where partners change, formats vary, and match volume is limited.

    OSR is open by design. Every formula, every weighting, and every adjustment threshold is explainable to any player, administrator, or partner. There are no black boxes.

    How Ratings Work

    Every match updates your rating automatically. The system considers not just whether you won, but how convincingly you won — set margins, game differentials, and how surprising the result was given the teams' ratings.

    Bigger upsets earn more points. Dominant victories earn more than narrow wins. Playing more matches builds confidence in your rating over time.

    Match Types

    Men's Ladies Mixed All Sorts

    OSR handles any combination — two men, two women, mixed gender, or any All Sorts configuration. Mixed and All Sorts pairs use a weighted rating that gives more weight to the stronger partner, reflecting their greater influence on the match.

    Your Level

    Your Level is simply your rating divided by 200. It gives you a simple number to communicate your standard without needing to explain raw rating points. Level 5 is entry, Level 9 is elite.

    Admin · Control Room

    Current individuals

    Individuals are the ingredients pairs are built from. Editing a seed rating re-derives any pairs they belong to on the next recompute.

    Add an individual

    Bulk add individuals (paste from Excel)

    Paste rows of Name, Gender, Rating, PIN — one per line. Tabs (straight from Excel) or commas. Gender = M or F. Rating optional — blank defaults to 1500 (M) / 1300 (F).

    Reset initial ratings

    Set every man's or lady's starting rating to one value and recompute the whole club. Handy when a club's first ratings need a clean restart. Each player's number can still be fine-tuned individually afterwards.

    Club name

    Change the club name shown on the front page (e.g. “OPR Gen 1” → “OPR Worcester”). Requires the master PIN.

    Create a pair

    Pick two individuals. The pair is born as one rated member: name A→Z, category from genders (Men's / Ladies / Mixed), initial rating = stronger×0.6 + weaker×0.4.

    Pick two individuals to preview the birth.

    Bulk create pairs (paste)

    Paste rows of Person A, Person B, PIN — one pair per line. Names must exactly match registered individuals. Category & 60/40 rating are computed automatically.

    Re-derive all pairs from current seeds

    Recomputes every pair's initial rating = 60/40 of its two components' current individual seeds, overwrites the stored initial, then recomputes the whole club. Use this to fix any drift after editing individual seeds. Rarely needed — it moves ratings across the board.

    Current pairs

    Each pair is one rated entity. "Move" = current − initial (since inception).
