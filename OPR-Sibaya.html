Install the new OPR on your phone

Two minutes, once. Swap the old app for the new one.

1

Remove the old OPR

Press and hold the old OPR icon on your home screen.

Android: choose Uninstall (or drag it to Uninstall).

iPhone: choose Remove App, then Delete.

2

On Android

Open the link above in Chrome.

Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).

Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.

3

On iPhone

Open the link above in Safari.

Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).

Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

That's it. Tap the new OPR icon, open your group, switch on Make this my home page, and you're set.
